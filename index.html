settlement.hlp — The six rules of the dock_□×
Atomic

One transaction

Tokens travel one direction and SOL the other inside the same transaction. If one leg fails, the whole thing reverts.

Signatures

Both sides sign

Buyer and seller approve the identical transaction, after each wallet shows a simulation of the outcome.

Custody

No operator funds

Package never takes hold of tokens or SOL and never requests a spending allowance.

Offers

Offers are not transfers

An offer is published terms plus a signed message. Your balance is untouched until you sign the swap.

Fees

No platform fee

The taker covers the Solana network fee and any token-account rent. Nothing else is added.

Limits

Full lot only

A lot is taken whole. There are no partial fills and no price changes after posting.

readme.hlp — Package help_□×
What is Package?

An OTC dock for Solana tokens. A holder puts a whole bag up as one lot with one total price in SOL, and a single buyer takes all of it in a wallet-to-wallet swap.

Why not just market-sell?

A pool reprices against you as you sell, so a large bag earns less on every chunk and the chart takes the hit. A fixed-price lot pays the full total you set and never touches the pool.

Who can take my offer?

A public lot can be taken by any wallet that pays the full total. A private lot is only reachable through its link, so only whoever you send it to can open and take it.

What does it cost?

Package charges no platform fee. The taker pays the normal Solana network fee and, if they've never held the token, the rent for a new token account.

What am I signing when I post?

A plain text message listing your terms. It proves the offer came from your wallet. It is not a transaction and gives nobody permission to move funds.

What happens when an offer expires?

It drops off the book and can no longer be taken. Nothing needs to be returned, because nothing ever left your wallet.

Does Package work with pump.fun coins?

Yes. It is built around them. Paste any mint ending in "pump" and Package applies pump.fun's fixed 1,000,000,000 supply and 6 decimals, shows the lot as a share of supply, and tells you whether the coin is still on the bonding curve or has graduated to PumpSwap.

How does $Package itself launch?

On pump.fun, the standard way: 1,000,000,000 supply, 6 decimals, sold from the bonding curve with no presale and no team allocation, graduating to PumpSwap once the curve fills at roughly 85 SOL. The contract address is posted on X at launch.

Can I change the price later?

No. The terms are sealed by your signature. Cancel the lot from Portfolio and post a fresh one.
